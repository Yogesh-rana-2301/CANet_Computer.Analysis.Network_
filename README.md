## How to use this repo

1. Pick one topic folder from the list below.
2. Read the concept checklist and write your own short notes.
3. Complete the practice drills in that topic.
4. Solve interview questions from `practice/interview-questions`.
5. Repeat with timed revision every week.

## Placement strategy

- Target understanding first, then speed.
- Practice subnetting daily for 10 to 15 minutes.
- Keep one-page summaries for each topic.
- Explain one concept out loud every day (teaching method).
- Do at least two mock interview rounds per week.
