# Types of Network (LAN, WAN, MAN)

Networks are classified by geographic scope and ownership.

## 1. LAN (Local Area Network)

A LAN is a network in a limited area such as:

- Home
- Office floor
- School lab
- Small campus block

Typical properties:

- High speed
- Low latency
- Usually privately managed
- Common technologies: Ethernet, Wi-Fi

Examples:

- Office PCs connected through switches
- Home Wi-Fi with router and smart devices

## 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A MAN connects multiple LANs across a city or large campus area.

Typical properties:

- Medium-to-high speed
- Covers larger area than LAN, smaller than WAN
- Often operated by telecom providers, government, or large institutions

Examples:

- University network spanning multiple city buildings
- City-wide public service network

## 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

A WAN connects networks over large geographic distances (country/global).

Typical properties:

- Higher latency than LAN
- Uses leased lines, MPLS, internet VPNs, submarine fiber, satellite links
- Managed by service providers and enterprises

Examples:

- Branch offices connected across different cities
- The internet itself (largest WAN)

## LAN vs MAN vs WAN (Comparison)

| Feature      | LAN                      | MAN                      | WAN                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Coverage     | Small area               | City-level               | Country/global                 |
| Ownership    | Single organization/home | Institution/provider     | Multiple providers/orgs        |
| Speed        | Usually highest          | High to medium           | Medium to variable             |
| Latency      | Lowest                   | Low to medium            | Highest among three            |
| Cost per bit | Lowest                   | Medium                   | Higher                         |
| Typical use  | Office/home networking   | City campus interconnect | Inter-city/global connectivity |

## Which One Is Used Where?

- LAN: internal office communication and local servers
- MAN: city branch interconnection
- WAN: global app access, branch-to-HQ links, internet

## Interview Tip

If asked quickly:

- LAN = local and fast
- MAN = metropolitan/city scope
- WAN = long-distance and large-scale
